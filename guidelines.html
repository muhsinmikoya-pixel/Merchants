Marketplace guidelines

MERCHANT helps customers discover independent stores and helps sellers receive orders. It is a marketplace tool, not a party to the sale between a customer and a seller.

1. Trade honestly

Sellers must describe products accurately, use genuine photos where possible, keep prices and stock current, and communicate clearly. Customers must provide accurate contact and delivery information.

2. Payments and delivery

Customers and sellers must agree directly on payment, delivery, collection, refunds and any other sale terms before completing a trade. Check the seller and the product details before sending money. Do not send money to an account you cannot verify.

3. Your decisions and responsibility

Each customer and seller is responsible for checking the other party, making their own trade decisions, communicating honestly and meeting their own commitments. Do not blame MERCHANT for a user's own choice to send money, accept an offer, provide incorrect details or fail to keep a promise. MERCHANT does not act as the buyer, seller, payment processor or delivery provider, and does not control private arrangements between users. Nothing here removes rights or responsibilities that applicable law gives you or MERCHANT.

4. Prohibited behaviour

No scams, impersonation, stolen or counterfeit goods, illegal or dangerous products, threats, harassment, misleading listings or attempts to move users into unsafe transactions.

5. Reports and reviews

Customers can report a seller or listing they believe breaks these guidelines. Give truthful information and include useful details. MERCHANT may review reports and restrict accounts or listings where appropriate. A report is not a guaranteed refund or a decision about a private dispute.

6. Account safety

Keep your sign-in details private. Use accurate account information and contact MERCHANT through the available support channel if you notice suspicious activity.
